Munshi is a WhatsApp bookkeeping assistant operated by Nodeable AI LLC ("we", "us", or "our"). This privacy policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

What Munshi does

Munshi helps small business operators keep business records by talking to a WhatsApp contact. You can send text messages and voice notes. Munshi uses those messages to create and update bookkeeping records.

Information we collect

When you message Munshi on WhatsApp, we receive the content you send, including text messages and voice notes, along with related WhatsApp metadata such as your phone number and message timestamps. We do not request access to your contacts, camera, or other WhatsApp data beyond the messages you send to Munshi.

How we use your information

We process the messages and voice notes you send with AI, using Google Gemini, to extract business records such as production, sales, payments, and customer accounts. We store those records in Google Sheets owned by the operator or the user of the service. We use this information only to provide the Munshi bookkeeping service, to improve accuracy, and to respond to your requests, including data deletion requests.

We do not sell your data. We do not use your messages or business records to advertise to you.

Service providers

WhatsApp and Meta Platforms process the messages you send to Munshi in order to deliver them to us. They act as processors (and, in some cases, as independent controllers of WhatsApp account data under their own policies). We use Google Gemini to analyze message content and extract records, and Google Sheets to store those records. These providers process data only as needed to operate Munshi, or as described in their own terms.

Data retention

We keep your messages, extracted records, and related data until you ask us to delete them, or until we no longer need them to provide the service.

How to request deletion

You can ask us to delete your data at any time by:

  • Emailing rizwan@nodeable.ai, or
  • Sending the message "delete my data" to Munshi on WhatsApp

We will delete the personal data and business records we hold about you, except where we must keep limited information to comply with law or to complete a request you already made. More detail is on our Data Deletion page.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email Nodeable AI LLC at rizwan@nodeable.ai.
