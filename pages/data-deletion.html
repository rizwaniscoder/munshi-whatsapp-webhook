This page explains how to request deletion of data associated with Munshi, a WhatsApp bookkeeping assistant operated by Nodeable AI LLC. Meta and WhatsApp users can use this URL when they need a data deletion callback or instructions page.

How to request deletion

You can request deletion in either of these ways:

  • Email rizwan@nodeable.ai with the WhatsApp phone number you used with Munshi and the subject or message "delete my data".
  • Send the message "delete my data" to Munshi on WhatsApp from the same account you used with the service.

What we delete

When we confirm your request, we delete the messages and voice notes we received from you, the business records extracted from them, and related data stored in the Google Sheets used by Munshi, as well as any copies we hold for operating the service.

What may remain

We may retain limited information if we are required to do so by law, or if we need it to complete a request or resolve a dispute. WhatsApp and Meta may also retain message data according to their own policies, which we do not control.

Timing

We will process confirmed deletion requests promptly and will email you at the address you provide, or reply on WhatsApp, when the deletion is complete.
